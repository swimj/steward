# Chewing

## Motivation, discipline, and whom the product serves

Justin's starting intuition is a learner with meaningful motivation already present. He worries that artificial motivation can establish routines while limiting the learner's eventual potential. If someone does not want to learn something, there are other worthwhile ways to spend a life.

The complication: short-term wishes and long-term interests can conflict. Some learners may want support that keeps them connected to an ambition they still care about.

Open questions:
- What should the app do when someone arrives without a particular interest that day, or keeps exploring while earlier learning fades?
- What kind of encouragement or discipline would feel welcome and serve the learner's own goals?
- Should learners explicitly choose that support, should the app infer preferences over time, or some combination? What would justify those inferences?
- Is a desire for greater access to the world through language a useful audience boundary, while allowing energy and discipline to vary?

Counterpoint raised by the steward, still to explore: artificial structure is not necessarily artificial motivation. A reminder or routine can serve a personal ambition; the concern is whether maintaining the routine displaces that ambition.

## Retention and a sustainable business

Justin's business hypothesis: familiar motivational or retention mechanisms might give people enough time to appreciate a different model of learning, even if those mechanisms are not the ideal route. Some learners may ultimately leave, and that can be acceptable. The bet is that enough people value the core offering to sustain the business; not everyone needs to be a customer.

Open questions:
- Which retention mechanisms help learners discover lasting value, and which would undermine the openness of the product?
- How would we tell whether retention is supporting learning or becoming an end in itself?
- Can the product welcome successful departures while sustaining a business around people who continue to benefit?

These are unresolved questions and hypotheses, not selected features or experiments.
