# Product vision

Learning serves the learner's life. The product should help people venture into the world and use their language skills as best they can, with greater freedom and capability as they grow.

The experience should feel open. There is always room to ask, “What would you like to do?” The learner brings their own interests, experiences, and reasons for learning; the app welcomes that direction rather than confining them to an all-encompassing learning platform.

It should be a familiar place to return to: somewhere to share an experience, make sense of a difficulty, and find something actionable to work on. A learner might bring a 小红书 post and comment thread whose subject fascinates them but whose intricacies are hard to follow. The app helps them stretch toward it, or offers a more digestible way into the same themes. Their interest remains the destination even when the next step needs to be smaller.

That openness has continuity behind it. The app remembers what the learner has worked on and helps important takeaways take root over time. Exploration can accumulate into lasting capability, with progress and reinforcement supporting the learner's purposes.

The aim is a richer relationship with the world through language. Continued dependence on the app is not the ultimate measure of success.
